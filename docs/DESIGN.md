# Poglite design contract

## North star

**Slam the stack. Face-up POGs activate. Defeat the enemy before it defeats you.**

The game may become content-rich, but that sentence must remain substantially true.

## Complexity budget

Complexity belongs in:

- which POGs and slammers drop;
- rarity and tiers;
- authored POG effects;
- compatible slammer affixes;
- enemy attack patterns;
- run rewards and pacing;
- collection art and print variants.

Complexity does **not** belong in extra random damage rolls after the slam. Physics is already the randomizer.

## Combat vocabulary

Initial vocabulary:

- HP
- Power
- Guard
- face-up activation

Everything else has to earn its way into the game through playtesting.

## Loot rule

**Rich generator, tiny tooltip.**

A reward may have deep generation logic underneath it, but a player should understand why it is useful from one or two short lines.

## Content rule

**Unlimited content, limited rules.**

Prefer a new combination of existing primitives over a new subsystem.
